# Groups have no owner and Members can't be removed

A Group has no owner or admin. Any Member can create Invite Links to it, set Checkpoints and change its Group Entries, and nobody can remove another Member: people only leave of their own accord. weebs is for small groups of friends who already trust each other, so roles and kicking would add friction and social awkwardness for little benefit. The accepted risk is that an unwanted Member can't be got rid of; this is limited by joining only being possible through a single-use Invite Link that expires after a week.

## Considered Options

- **Creator is the Owner**: the Owner invites and removes Members. Rejected because it makes one friend the gatekeeper and needs ownership hand-over when they leave.
